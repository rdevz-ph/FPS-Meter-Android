info Why Support Matters

FPS Meter Android is independently maintained. Your support helps cover:

- Testing on diverse hardware platforms including Qualcomm Snapdragon and MediaTek devices.
- Maintaining real-time SurfaceFlinger sampling and Shizuku privileged telemetry across new Android OS releases.
- Keeping the app completely ad-free, lightweight, and offline-first for everyone.
